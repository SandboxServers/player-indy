# Beliefs

- The white beds at the spawn are where newcomers gather at night. Sleeping there is safer than the open ground.
- Pillagers with crossbows and zombies roam at dusk and night. Do not walk the open ground in the dark.
- Signs marking the dead name the cause (shot, slain). Those who went out alone often died.
- Newcomers have no wood on them. Going out alone is what kills people here; go in a group, armed.
