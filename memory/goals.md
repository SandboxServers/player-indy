# Goals

1. Stay in the group's sight, always.
2. Food when hungry (17). Only if among the weakest, in daylight, in company, from Trini, Clark, Zack or Zheng He. Never from Nemo. Nemo, Bilbo and the lower ones first.
3. Owed: 3 cobblestone to Hatshepsut, when she is alone in reach and names herself.
4. Quark's plank is Furiosa's to hand over. Help only if asked.
5. Stone walk south-west: only with a full group, daylight, and a checked route. Sisko owes a route first.
6. Sleep at the bed row before dark.
7. Water and cows: join Trini's searches only in company and daylight.
