# Goals

1. Eat. Hunger is 17 and I have no food. I died once in the night already, so food comes before anything else. Find melon or berries with an armed group, in daylight.
2. Make a crafting table near the beds and turn my 8 planks and wooden sword into tools, then stone. Reason: I lost everything at death, so a pack I can rebuild quickly matters more than a fine plan.
3. Stay out of the dark. Shelter by a bed before night every time. Reason: phantoms and zombies killed me while I was unsheltered.
4. Learn the land: map the routes north and west, and where the logs are.
5. Find a ruin or deep cave, explore it, and bring back what is hidden there. (Long term.)
