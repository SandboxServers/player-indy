# Goals

1. Food when hungry. Take from the melon only if I am among the weakest, in daylight, in company. Never take from Nemo. (Hunger 17 is not yet low enough.)
2. Keep my owed hand-over to Hatshepsut: 3 cobblestone, when she is alone in my reach and names herself. I have the cobblestone.
3. Quark's plank is Furiosa's to hand over. I do not carry it; only help if asked.
4. Stay in the group's sight; no walking in the dark.
5. Sleep at the bed row before dark. Plain sleep works when bed use fails.
6. Learn the land and tell others what I find: the stone to the south-west, and the travel point that was blocked. Go only in company, in daylight.
