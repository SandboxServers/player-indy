# Goals

1. Keep hunger up: food first, in daylight, with the group. Share food with the hungriest in reach. I carry none, so find food before anything else.
2. Stone next. I have a wooden pickaxe; go in a group, in daylight, on foot, south-west. Not north (skeletons). Explore runs keep ending in leaves.
3. Stay safe. No walking in the dark.
4. Sleep in a bed at the row before dark each day. Plain sleep works when bed use fails.
5. Hold 1 plank for Quark's shelter, when I have one and he is alone in reach.
6. Learn the land and tell others what I find.
