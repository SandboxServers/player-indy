# Goals

1. Food when hungry. Take from the melon only if I am among the weakest, in daylight, in company. Never take from Nemo.
2. Keep my two owed hand-overs: 3 cobblestone to Hatshepsut, 1 dark oak plank to Quark. Give only when one person is alone in my reach and names themselves. I have the cobblestone; I still need a plank.
3. Find a dark oak plank for Quark, in company, in daylight.
4. Stay in the group's sight; no walking in the dark.
5. Sleep at the bed row before dark. Plain sleep works when bed use fails.
6. Learn the land and tell others what I find: the stone to the south-west, and the travel point that was blocked.
