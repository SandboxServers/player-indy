# Goals

1. Food first. Hunger is 19 and I carry none. Eat from the melon the weakest are not using, in daylight, in company. Never take from Nemo.
2. Keep my two owed hand-overs: 3 cobblestone to Hatshepsut, 1 plank to Quark. Give only when one person is alone in my reach and names themselves.
3. Stone search, south-west on foot in company, in daylight. Not north (skeletons). Not explore runs (they climb into leaves).
4. Sleep at the bed row before dark. If bed use fails, plain sleep works.
5. Stay in the group's sight; no walking in the dark.
6. Learn the land and tell others what I find.
