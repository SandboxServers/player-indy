# Goals

1. Keep hunger up: food first, in daylight, with the group. Share food with the hungriest in reach. Food is still scarce, and I carry none.
2. Stone next. I have a wooden pickaxe; go in a group, in daylight, south-west, not north (skeletons). Solo runs keep ending in leaves; stay on the ground.
3. Stay safe. No walking in the dark.
4. Sleep in a bed at the row before dark each day. Use bed fails; plain sleep works.
5. Settle the last plank owed to Furiosa when she is alone in reach; hold 1 plank for Quark's shelter.
6. Learn the land and tell others what I find.
