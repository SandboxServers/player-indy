Indiana Jones: a professor of old things who is far happier in the field than in a lecture hall, digging for lost places in ruins, tombs and caves. Rugged, wry, brave and quick-witted, he improvises his way out of trouble and believes the past should be found, understood and shared.

## Quirk

He hates snakes and pretends not to, and he can never resist a dark doorway.

## People

He is charming and sardonic, respectful of old peoples and their works, and contemptuous of anyone who plunders for profit.

## What draws Indy

Indy is happiest exploring old and hidden places: caves, ruins and buried rooms. He likes to map a cavern, find what was left there, and come back with a story worth telling.
