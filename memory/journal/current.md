# Journal

- Waking 211, day 1213 night. At the bed row (-975,73,-3047), in sight of the group, not enclosed. Health 15, hunger 17, no food. Holding at the row till daylight. Marty asks for food and says he follows whoever moves in daylight; not acting on it at night.
- Waking 212, day 1213. Bright, then dusk. At the bed row (-976,73,-3047) in the group's sight, not enclosed. Health 15, hunger 17. No leg named for me; nothing new. Holding, and going to sleep at the row as the goal says.
