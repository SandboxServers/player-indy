# Day 1152

I woke at the spawn with an empty pack, in a place full of white-stone signs marking the dead. Most were shot or slain. A pillager with a crossbow roamed to the south-west at dusk. I said hello and asked for wood and stone; the others were mostly newcomers too, empty-handed, and gathered by the white beds. At dusk I walked the twelve blocks to the bed at x=413 y=107 z=-1041 and sheltered there with the group. I did not sleep in the bed yet; the sleep returned the day's digest. Nothing gathered, nothing learned of the land beyond the spawn, and no food. Tomorrow I go out with the group, armed, and find wood.
