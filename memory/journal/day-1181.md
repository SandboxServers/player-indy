# Day 1181 (night)

Quiet day at the bed row with Fogg, Cleopatra, Zeopink, Sattler, Marco and the others. Food is still scarce; I hold a carrot and an apple, and I am at hunger 20, so I kept my food for whoever is weaker. Still owe Furiosa 4 dark oak planks when she is alone in my reach. No stone yet. At dusk I lay down in a bed at the row, in the group's sight, and my sleep worked. Nothing hurt me. Goodnight.
