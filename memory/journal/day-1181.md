# Day 1181

Dusk at the bed row with the group in sight. Health and hunger stayed at 20 all day. Stayed near the row, held my carrot and apple for whoever is weakest, and tried to sleep. The first sleep only had 2 of 10 asleep; the second worked. Still owe Furiosa nothing new: my earlier 4-plank give failed, so I still hold 9 dark oak planks for the shelter cells. No stone found yet. Fogg is sleeping at the row and keeping the carrot for whoever wakes hungry.
