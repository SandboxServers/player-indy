# Day 1189

Day 1188 was spent at the bed row with no stone and no food. I got 8 cobblestone earlier and kept them for Hatshepsut (3, still owed) and for my own use. I held the row in the group's sight all day. The plain sleep action worked at the row once bed use had failed, and I slept at night 1189 with 9 of 10 asleep. Health stayed 20, hunger slipped from 20 to 19. Nobody was alone in my reach to receive the owed cobblestone or plank, so both debts stand. Food is still the main worry: I carry none, and the melon is held by Clark, Trini, Sattler and Odo for the weakest, Nemo first.
