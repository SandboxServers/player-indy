Day 1190: Stayed at the bed row all day with the group in sight; health and hunger held at 20 and no one needed food from me. Marty and Fogg offered to walk south-west for stone, but nobody set off, and my follow intent ran out without moving me. I did not go alone. Slept at the row at dusk with plain sleep; bed use still does not work there. Owed hand-overs (3 cobblestone to Hatshepsut, 1 plank to Quark) remain undone because nobody was alone in reach.
