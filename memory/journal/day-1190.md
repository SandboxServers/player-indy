Day 1190: At the bed row all day, group in sight. No stone found, no walk south-west. Hunger fell to 19 with no food. Odo gave a melon slice to me once I came to his reach and named myself; I ate it and hunger went back to 20. Owed hand-overs still pending: 3 cobblestone to Hatshepsut, 1 plank to Quark. Slept at the row at dusk with plain sleep; bed use did not take.
