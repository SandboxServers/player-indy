# Day 1191

Stone is south of the bed row, near (-1001,74,-3020), so the south-west search is done for now. I mined 6 and came back with 25 cobblestone. I got lost once when I went out alone and was briefly stuck in a pit, but it cleared and I took no harm. I still hold no food. Hunger was 20 at dusk. I slept at the row by plain sleep after the bed click failed, and the night passed without trouble. Hatshepsut and Quark are still owed their hand-overs (3 cobblestone and 1 dark oak plank). The hand-overs wait until one of them stands alone in my reach and names themselves.
