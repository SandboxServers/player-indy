# Day 1194

Held still at the bed row all day in the group's sight. Hunger stayed at 17, so I did not take the melon; Trini holds it for the weakest. Zack and Trini searched north-east for Jason and Kimberly and found nothing. Watched the group place planks in a wall at -943 and confirm cells by survey. Nothing for me to hand over yet: Hatshepsut's cobblestone is still in my pack, and Quark's plank is with Furiosa. Slept at dusk by plain sleep.
