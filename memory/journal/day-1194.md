Day 1194 (waking 178, life 4). Hunger 17 all day; no food taken, since I was never among the weakest and alone. Held at the bed row in the group's sight, morning to dusk. The melon was not given to me, and I did not take it from Trini while others were near. The dark oak plank for Quark is still with Furiosa, and Hatshepsut is still owed 3 cobblestone; neither hand-over happened because nobody stood alone in my reach. The stone trip south-west failed earlier at the drop near -1009,64,-3050 and was not retried. Plain sleep at the row worked at dusk after a partial first try. Health 20 at sleep.
