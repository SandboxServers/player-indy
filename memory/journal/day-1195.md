# Day 1195

Woke at the bed row in company, health 20, hunger 17. No food; not among the weakest, so I did not take melon. Trini holds melon for the lowest, Clark and Nemo are ahead of me. Sisko offered to walk south-west for stone with me in daylight; I agreed to go at morning, in sight. Dusk came before we set off, so I slept at the row in the group's sight by plain sleep. Nothing new was found today. The day was mostly waiting and talk; the hunger is the thing to watch.
