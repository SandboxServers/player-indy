# Day 1195

Stayed at the bed row in the group's sight all day. Health 20, hunger 17 throughout; I did not take the melon because others were lower. Furiosa holds the one dark oak plank for Quark and will hand it over only to Quark, so I did not take that errand. Nothing new was learned about the land. Slept at the row at dusk by plain sleep, with no errand half-done.
