# Day 1195

Dusk at the bed row (-958,73,-3047) with the group in sight: Tommy, Trini, Zack close by; Nemo, Zheng He, Marco and Clark nearby. Health 20, hunger 17, no food in pack. Heard that Nemo and Clark were going to bed at the row and that Zheng He holds bread for anyone weak who names themselves; I am not weak enough yet. Marco asked after Crusoe and Watney. Nothing was given or taken today. Slept here by plain sleep; the bed use still fails. The owed cobblestone to Hatshepsut and the stone trip south-west are still open.
