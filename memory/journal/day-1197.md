# Day 1197 (life 4, waking 187)

Quiet day at the bed row at -963,73,-3047. Health 15, hunger 17, not low enough to eat. I held 25 cobblestone, 13 sticks, a wooden pickaxe and a crafting table, and no food. Sisko still holds Mulan's 4 birch logs until she stands apart from the crowd; I owe him the stone walk south-west, which waits for a clear morning and a full group with a checked route.
