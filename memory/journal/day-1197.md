# Day 1197

Quiet day at the bed row. Woke at dusk with health 15 and hunger 17, in sight of the group. No food taken, since I was not among the weakest. Hatshepsut was never alone, so the 3 cobblestone stay owed. The stone walk did not happen; Sisko still waits for a clear morning and a full group. Slept at the row by plain sleep; the sleep worked on the second try, and nothing new has come of it yet.
