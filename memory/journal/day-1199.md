# Day 1199

Stayed at the bed row all day in the group's sight, health 15, hunger 17, no food taken. Watched Mulan, Quark, Odo and Harriet wall one cell at -962,73,-3054 after a floor hole at y72 was found and filled; the careful survey-and-confirm habit worked well. I placed nothing. The stone walk south-west still has no checked route, so I did not go. Food stayed with Clark, Trini and Zheng He for the weakest. Slept at the row by plain sleep.
