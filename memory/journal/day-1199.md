# Day 1199

Waking 191 at the bed row, health 15, hunger 17, no food. Food was short for the whole group. Trini and Clark shared melon and carrot first with the lowest (Aragorn, Billy), which was right, and I kept to my rule of taking food only when among the weakest. Harriet and Mulan scouted west for cows and sheep and found none. My rest intent failed once, but I stayed at the row in the group's sight all day. Slept at the row at dusk, in sight of others, with no errand half-done.
