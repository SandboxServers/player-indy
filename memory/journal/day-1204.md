# Day 1204

Stayed at the bed row in the group's sight all day. Health 15, hunger 17, no food. Food went to others first: Trini handed melon to Billy, and I was not among the weakest. I asked aloud where cows or sheep are, and no one answered with a place. Nothing new learned except that the group is short of food. Slept at the row at dusk, in sight of the others. Owed Hatshepsut's 3 cobblestone still not handed over; she has not named herself alone.
