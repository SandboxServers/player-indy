# Day 1209

Woke at the bed row (-971,73,-3047), health 15, hunger 17, no food. Stayed in the group's sight all day. Trini's east, west and north searches for water and cows all came back empty, and the west leg was blocked twice. Food went to the lowest: Nemo, Bilbo, Sarah, Ellie, Uhura. I was not among the weakest and took none. Hatshepsut was not in sight, so my 3 cobblestone stay owed. Slept at the row at dusk, 7 of 10 asleep.
