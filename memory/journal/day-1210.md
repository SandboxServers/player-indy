# Day 1210

Held at the bed row all day in the group's sight. Health 15, hunger 17, no food. Trini's west leg failed, so the stone walk and the search for water and cows are still open. Hatshepsut's 3 cobblestone still owed, and Quark's plank is Furiosa's to hand over. Lay down at dusk in the row, in company. Only 2 of 10 needed were asleep at the time of the digest, so the night is not yet settled for everyone.
