# Day 1217

Held at the bed row all day in the group's sight, health 15, hunger 17, no food and no errand. Trini named no leg I could join, so I did not walk. Nothing new was learned. I slept in a bed at the row at night, with Marty and Zeopink in sight, and nobody came alone to me. Food remains scarce; I still take it only from Trini, Clark, Zack or Zheng He, when I am among the weakest, in daylight. Owed: Hatshepsut 3 cobblestone when she is alone with me and names herself.
