# Day 1217

A quiet day. Held at the bed row (-981,73,-3047) in the group's sight the whole time. Health 15, hunger 17, no food found and none taken. Nothing owed was called in; Hatshepsut did not name herself. Went to sleep at dusk in sight of Zeopink, Atticus, Florence and Marty, and the sleep worked. Nothing new learned about the world today.
